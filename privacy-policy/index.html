1. Who We Are

Timbertop Fencing Pty Ltd is a fencing and retaining wall business based on the Sunshine Coast, Queensland. We provide expert fencing solutions including Colorbond, aluminium, timber fencing, and retaining walls.

2. What Personal Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address, and property address
  • Enquiry details: information you provide in your message or enquiry form
  • Call and site-visit notes: records of conversations and site assessments related to your project
  • Cookie and device data: information collected through Google advertising tags, including device identifiers and website activity

3. How We Collect Your Information

We collect personal information through:

  • Website enquiry forms
  • Phone calls and text messages
  • Email correspondence
  • On-site visits and consultations
  • Automated technologies such as cookies and device identifiers placed by Google advertising tags

4. Why We Collect Your Information

We collect and use your personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and prepare quotes for fencing or retaining wall projects
  • Schedule and complete work on your property
  • Send invoices and process payments
  • Follow up on enquiries and maintain our customer relationships
  • Measure and improve the performance of our advertising campaigns
  • Show relevant advertising to people who may be interested in our services

5. Who We Share Your Information With

We share your personal information with the following third parties as necessary to provide our services:

Formspree (Form Hosting Provider)

Your enquiry form submissions are processed and stored by Formspree, a form hosting service. Formspree stores your information on servers hosted by Amazon Web Services (AWS) in the United States of America.

Google (Advertising and Measurement)

We share a scrambled (hashed) copy of your contact details (email and phone number) with Google to measure how our advertising performs. Google also uses cookies and device identifiers to track website visits for advertising purposes. Google may process this information in the United States of America and other countries where Google operates.

Other Service Providers

We may share your information with:

  • Suppliers and subcontractors as needed to complete your project
  • Our accountant or bookkeeping software providers for invoicing and financial management
  • Other service providers necessary to deliver our fencing and retaining wall services

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Overseas Disclosure of Your Information

Your personal information may be disclosed to or stored in the following overseas locations:

  • United States of America: through Formspree (form hosting on AWS servers) and Google (advertising measurement and analytics)
  • Other countries: where Google operates data processing facilities

7. Google Advertising and Your Privacy

We use Google Ads to advertise our fencing services online. This involves:

How Third-Party Vendors Use Your Data

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, show our ads on websites across the internet
  • Google and other vendors use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on your past visits to our website
  • We share a scrambled (hashed) version of your contact details with Google to measure how many people who see or click our ads go on to contact us

Remarketing and Personalised Advertising

Google may use your website visit information to show you personalised advertising for our services when you browse other websites. This is called remarketing or interest-based advertising.

How to Opt Out

You can control and opt out of personalised advertising in the following ways:

  • Google Ads Settings: Visit https://myadcenter.google.com/ to manage your Google ad preferences and opt out
  • Network Advertising Initiative: Visit https://optout.networkadvertising.org/ to opt out of personalised ads from participating ad networks
  • Device settings: You can also adjust ad tracking settings on your mobile device or browser

8. How We Store and Secure Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, unauthorised access, modification, or disclosure. This includes:

  • Using secure third-party service providers (Formspree, Google) with appropriate security measures
  • Restricting access to personal information to authorised personnel only
  • Using secure communication channels where appropriate

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services and respond to your enquiry
  • Complete any work you engage us to perform
  • Comply with legal, accounting, and tax obligations (typically 7 years for financial records)
  • Manage our customer relationships and follow up on past enquiries

Once your information is no longer needed, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.

10. Access, Correction, and Deletion of Your Information

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any personal information that is inaccurate, out of date, or incomplete
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal obligations to retain certain records

To make a request, please contact us using the details at the top of this page.

11. How to Make a Complaint

If you have a concern or complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us first using the contact details at the top of this page. We will investigate your complaint and respond to you as soon as practicable.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner:

  • Website: https://www.oaic.gov.au/
  • Phone: 1300 363 992
  • Email: enquiries@oaic.gov.au

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
